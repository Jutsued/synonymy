How to play Synonymy

Every day there's one target word in the middle of the screen, surrounded by 20 floating words. Your job is to tap the 5 true synonyms, the words that mean the same thing.

  • 5 synonyms are the answers. Find all five to win.
  • 5 antonyms mean the opposite. Easy to spot, easy to tap by accident.
  • 5 related words belong to the same scene but don't mean the same thing.
  • 5 bombs have nothing to do with the target at all.

You start with 5 energy charges. Every wrong tap costs one. Run out and the game ends. Win on consecutive days to build your streak, then share your result grid with friends.

Frequently asked questions

When does the new puzzle come out?

A new puzzle unlocks every day at midnight UTC (8 PM Eastern in summer, 7 PM in winter). The countdown at the top of the page shows exactly how long is left.

Is Synonymy free?

Yes. It's free to play, there's no account, and nothing to download. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

Does everyone get the same word?

Yes. Every player around the world gets the same puzzle each day, so you can compare results with friends.

Where is my streak saved?

Your progress and streak are saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your browser data resets them.
